# 1. Executive Result

Audit: Netzwerk neu A2 official sources

Audit date: 2026-08-21

Scope: official Ernst Klett Sprachen and Klett-operated platforms only.
Purpose: identify authoritative, technically usable sources for a later quality-controlled content intake. This audit does not authorize redistribution, import content, or modify learner data.

The current official Netzwerk neu series catalog exposes substantial A2 material: audio and video transcripts, Kursbuch and Übungsbuch solutions, German–Arabic and German–English glossaries, chapter vocabulary, chapter tests, online exercises, teacher boards, copy templates, Landeskunde worksheets, clip references, and pacing plans. All 12 chapters have usable official coverage.

This audit catalogs **47 public content records** and **22 licensed/access-controlled download or product records**. Streaming variants are not counted as import packages. Public availability is not evidence of permission to redistribute source content in DeutschFlow.

The local 189-file A2 audio inventory can be mapped deterministically to book, chapter, disc, and official track number from the official transcript PDFs. It cannot yet be mapped completely to printed page and exercise: **0 proven**, **189 partially proven**, and **0 wholly unresolved** local files under the strict classification in section 8. Page/exercise mapping remains unresolved for all 189 files.

Official solutions and official audio transcripts are available for Kursbuch and Übungsbuch, chapters 1–12. Kapitel 2, **Nach der Schulzeit**, is the recommended first controlled intake because its title and chapter identity are consistent across the official sources, its local audio ranges are complete, and it avoids the Kapitel 1 title inconsistency and missing local Übungsbuch track 1.1.

# 4. Publicly Importable Content

“Publicly importable” here means technically retrievable for controlled internal research and intake evaluation. It does **not** mean cleared for redistribution in DeutschFlow.

| Content type | What can be recovered | Technical suitability and guardrail |
|---|---|---|
| Audio transcripts | Official track labels, chapter boundaries, dialogue/listening text | Highest-confidence deterministic text source. Preserve source URL, track label, chapter, and extraction version. |
| Übungsbuch solutions | Chapter/task structure and official answers | Clean text layer; suitable for structured intake after rights and human QA. |
| Kursbuch solutions | Chapter/task structure and official answers | Extractable with layout-aware tooling such as PyMuPDF; naive raw PDF extraction can appear empty or malformed. |
| German–Arabic and German–English glossaries | Vocabulary, bilingual meanings, examples, chapter/task references | Valuable bilingual evidence. Typographic syllable highlighting fragments words in raw extraction; reconstruct layout and validate each record. |
| Kapitelwortschatz | Chapter vocabulary, examples, task references | Useful cross-check. Apply the same fragmented-word quality gate as the glossaries. |
| Plans and overviews | Chapter/page/task mappings, grammar, vocabulary, text types, online and clip references | Strong structural evidence; preserve edition identity with every page reference. |
| Online exercises | Interactive official exercise content for every chapter | Publicly usable as an official reference. Do not scrape or redistribute without rights review. |
| Teacher boards | One focused book task per chapter plus teaching notes/examples | Good chapter/task evidence and small-scope human QA source. Unnumbered player controls do not establish audio-file identity. |
| Copy templates | 119 ZIP entries containing PDFs/JPG role cards and some solutions | Public package, but mixed formats and quality; each file requires classification and rights/quality review. |
| Chapter tests | Twelve test PDFs and one answer-key PDF | Eleven test PDFs have usable text; Kapitel 5 and the answer key are image-only and require verified OCR/manual review. |
| Landeskunde worksheets | Exercises and cultural contexts for selected Austrian/Swiss topics | Official supplementary content; chapter relationship is explicit in filenames/catalog metadata. |

No source should be ingested by copying raw extracted text directly. Intake must retain provenance, verification state, content version, chapter/task/edition identity, and a recoverable source reference.
